# Privacy policy — DSPi Console for Android

*Effective 3 November 2026. Applies to the app "DSPi Console for Android"
(`se.bofr.dspi_console_android`) by Bofr5555.*

**In short: the app collects no personal data and sends nothing anywhere.** It has no internet
access at all (it does not request Android's network permission), no accounts, no ads, no analytics
and no crash reporting.

## What the app does with data

- **Your DSPi device.** The app talks to a DSPi audio processor connected to your phone over USB.
  It reads and changes the device's settings (filters, routing, volume, presets) and shows its live
  levels and status, including the device's serial number. This data stays between the phone and
  the device.
- **Settings stored on the phone.** App preferences (display options, graph settings, which input
  channels you linked, per device serial number) are stored in the app's private storage on your
  phone. They are not backed up to the cloud (Android backup is turned off for this app) and are
  removed when you uninstall the app.
- **Files you choose.** When you import or export presets or filter files, or install firmware from
  a file, the app reads or writes only the file you pick in Android's file picker. When you share a
  preset, a temporary copy is placed in the app's cache for the app you share it with.
- **Bundled data.** The AutoEQ headphone database and the official DSPi firmware images ship inside
  the app; using them involves no download.
- **Notifications.** While a DSPi is connected, the app shows a notification with volume, mute and
  next-preset controls (Android requires a visible notification for this). It contains nothing but the
  device status.
